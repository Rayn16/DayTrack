☀️ How DayTrack Works
✅ Tasks
Tap the + button to create a task. Choose One-time for a single-day task, or Recurring 🔄 to repeat it on specific days of the week (or every day). One-time tasks can get a day; future ones wait greyed out until then. Tap a task to mark it done, or swipe it right. Swipe left to move it to tomorrow. Tap ⋯ to edit, delete or reorder it. Tasks with a time sort into morning, afternoon and evening. Hold the + button to say a task ("dentist Tuesday at 3"). A recurring task can have a count (8 glasses of water): each tap adds one, ⋯ takes one off. Below your tasks are 🎯 Goals (a goal split into steps; Gwen checks in when one stalls) and 📝 Lists (groceries, movies… Gwen can add to them).
🔴 Priority
When adding a task, pick High, Medium, or Low priority. A colored dot appears on the task row so you can spot what matters most at a glance.
📋 Subtasks
Break a task into smaller steps by adding subtasks when creating it. Type a step and press Enter (or tap +). On the task list, tap the 📋 X/Y badge to expand and check off individual steps.
⏰ Reminders
Set a reminder At a time (fires once at a specific hour) or Every… 🔁 (repeats on an interval, e.g. every 2h). Reminders fire as push notifications even when the app is closed — just activate notifications in Settings first. On Android you can tap ✅ Done, ⏰ 10 min or 📅 Tomorrow right on the reminder. Shared text from another app becomes a task. Quiet Hours in Settings pause "Every…" reminders at night.
🔥 Streaks
Complete all of today's tasks to earn a day on your overall streak (shown at the top of the Tasks tab). Recurring tasks also show their own individual 🔥 streak badge — how many consecutive days in a row you've completed that specific task.
⏱️ Timer
Track time across multiple steps in one session. Tap + Add for each step, name it, then ▶ to start / ⏸ to pause / ✓ to lock it. Tap 💾 Save Session when done — it appears in Past Sessions and counts toward your Summary stats.
📅 Calendar
Tap any day to see a detailed breakdown: progress bar, which tasks were done vs not done, and a notes section. Colored dots on the calendar show activity: 🟢 green = all tasks done, 🟠 orange = partial, purple = tasks exist but none done. Switch to Week to see seven days at once and drag a task to another day.
⭐ Level
You're a game character. Every task you tick gives XP, and the words in its name decide which stats grow: push-ups train Arms and Chest, Quran trains Faith, studying trains Intellect. Pick the stats yourself under Levels up when you add a task. Finishing every task, goal steps and timer sessions give XP too. Tap a stat to see what trained it. Your body lights up each part (arms, chest, back, core, legs, heart) by its level. Skills is a skill tree: each branch is a path (calisthenics, cooking, money, Quran…), some unlock others, start one and its practice task joins your list, tap I can do it when you master a step. Quests give daily, weekly and monthly goals; claim the reward when one is done (Gwen's quest from her house is in there too). Buffs raise your XP (a streak, 7 h of sleep, a good mood, a weekly quest) and debuffs lower it (too little sleep, overdue tasks, a broken streak). Story shows every level-up and where the XP came from, plus achievements; some give a title you can wear under your name. Each week a boss appears in Quests and your XP knocks its HP down.

Every day a daily boss shows up at a random time (you choose the hours) and your phone rings like an alarm. Do its challenge within 30 minutes and tap Strike for XP and loot; if it escapes you get −10% XP that day. Chests, bosses and story chapters drop items (streak freeze, XP potion, quest reroll) into your Bag, where you also spend coins (1 per 10 XP) on items or real-life rewards you set. Every 5 levels gives a perk. Achievements unlock gear that adds XP. Tasks done within an hour of each other build a combo. Mark a task Hardcore (double XP, misses cost XP) or Mini boss (worth more the longer it waits). Also: a daily login reward, story chapters, a bounty board for your own quests, bad habits you're quitting, a monthly fitness test, personal records, a pet that grows when you show up, Gwen in your party, a weekly report card, you vs last month, seasons, a year heatmap and a one-line journal. Share your character card with 📤 Share card.
📊 Summary
See your stats at a glance: today's completion, overall streak, total time tracked, and session count. The This Week bar chart shows daily completion % for the last 7 days. The Activity heatmap shows the past 90 days — darker green means more tasks completed. Habits shows how often you did each recurring task in the last 30 days, and Mood shows the mood you picked on the Tasks tab each day. Sleep charts the bed and wake times you log each morning.
💜 Gwen
Chat with Gwen about your day. She sees today's tasks, what's coming up, your streak and your mood. Ask her to add a task or remind you and it shows up in your list. Tap 🎤 to talk to her out loud; she answers in her voice (🔊 in the chat turns it off). She also messages you first: a morning hello and an evening nudge if something's left, even with the app closed. She answers from your PC when it's on (💻) and from the cloud when it isn't (☁️). Set her up in Settings. 🖼 Postcards keeps the pictures she makes in her house, and 💻 PC shows if your PC and Gwen are on and can start her or lock it.
⚙️ Settings
Push Notifications — tap Activate to enable background reminders. Dark Mode — switch themes. Cloud Sync — back up automatically and open your data on another phone with your private code. Export — download a JSON backup of all your tasks, sessions, and notes. Import — restore from a backup file. Prayer Times — today's times from your location, an optional reminder at each, and Gwen keeps tasks off them. Privacy — ask for your fingerprint for the whole app or just Gwen's tab. Every Sunday at 8pm you'll receive a weekly recap notification.
